📊 Madhav Ecommerce Sales Dashboard</br>

About the Project</br>

This project analyzes ecommerce sales data using Power BI to evaluate revenue, profitability, customer purchases, payment preferences, and product performance. The report combines multiple business metrics into a single dashboard, helping users explore sales trends across products, customers, states, and time periods.

Overall Sales & Profit</br>
Monthly Profit Trend</br>
State-wise Sales Performance</br>
Customer Contribution</br>
Payment Mode Distribution</br>
Product Category Analysis</br>
Top Profit-Generating Products</br>
Quarter and State Filtering</br>

Key Findings</br>

Generated 438K in sales with 37K profit.</br>
Uttar Pradesh recorded the highest sales contribution.</br>
Cash on Delivery was the most frequently used payment method.</br>
Clothing accounted for the largest quantity sold.</br>
Printers delivered the highest product-level profit.</br>
Profit varied considerably across months, indicating seasonal sales patterns.</br>

Outcome</br>

The report helps evaluate business performance from financial, geographical, and customer perspectives, supporting better decisions related to product strategy, regional sales planning, and revenue optimization.
